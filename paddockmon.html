Paddockmon

Before Playa Court there was Paddockmon, a trading card game made for Ignition 2026. Every card was someone or something from the event: a participant, a memorable moment, or a camp.

1,200unique cards

The cards

Each card was drawn from a real person, moment or camp at Ignition. People found themselves as rats, rabbits and chickens, with an attack named after what they're known for and a line of flavour text only their friends would fully get.

Rarer cards had full-bleed art, and some came back signed by the person on them.

A game in the woods

A mirror made the board, and dice kept track of damage.

Each turn you draw, bench your Paddockmon, attach one energy and attack. Knock out your opponent's last Paddockmon, or take your last Prize Card, and you win.

Rules

The rules came in a small book. Tap a page to read it full size.

Cover Your turn
  1. 1) Draw a cardFrom your deck, at the start of every turn.
  2. 2) Bench cardsPut as many Paddockmon as you want onto your bench.
  3. 3) Attach energyOne energy card per turn, matching the type at the top right of the Paddockmon.
  4. 4) RetreatOnce per turn, discard energy equal to the retreat cost to swap your active Paddockmon with one from the bench.
  5. 5) AttackPay the attack's energy cost, then put damage dice on the opponent's active Paddockmon (damage ÷ 10). Your turn ends after you attack.
  6. Weakness and resistanceWeakness doubles the damage from a matching type. Resistance takes 40 off.
  7. Knock out and victoryA Paddockmon is knocked out when its damage reaches its HP. Knock out the last one, or take your last Prize Card, to win.
