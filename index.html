Become a card

Anyone 18 or older at unSCruz 2027 can become a card. It's free, and it's always opt-in.

  1. Sign up. Tell us your name or playa name, your camp, the class that fits you, and three things you're known for.
  2. Send a photo. It becomes your card's portrait.
  3. Change your mind any time before the print deadline, and you're out.

Sign up

Leave No Trace

A card on the ground is MOOP. Every pack is gifted in a box or pouch, and cards are printed on durable stock with no glitter or foil. The packaging and rules sheets are recyclable paper, so pack them out with your recycling.

Play at a table or in camp, not on open ground. If you find a card, pick it up, and bring it back to the Playa Court table so it can be gifted again.

Questions

Who can submit a card?

Anyone participating in unSCruz 2027 can submit. Priority will be given to those who help with the event, bring art, or create theme camps.

How many cards do I need to play?

5–6 is recommended (a standard match uses 6), but you can play with any amount! See Formats for shorter and longer matches.

Can kids play?

Yes! Only those 18 or older can sign up for a card, but all ages can play. Parental guidance is recommended due to potential themes related to performance arts or camp gifts. Nothing sexual, gory or illegal will be depicted.

Has this been done before?

Yes! Paddockmon was a card game art project at Ignition 2026, with 1,200 unique cards made from participants, memorable moments and camps. Playa Court follows on from it, and some people, like ISHYne and Jonny 5, are in both. See Paddockmon.

Is this affiliated with unSCruz?

Playa Court is an independent, participant-made project. It is not produced, sponsored or endorsed by unSCruz or Burning Man Project, and any names or marks belong to their owners.

How to play

Two players, one table, about two minutes. The standard match is best of 5 rounds.

Show the rulesHide the rules
6 cardsOne more than the number of rounds, so even the last round is a choice.
5 tokensBeads, bottle caps, anything. One per round, in a pile in the middle.
1 strangerOr a friend. Sit across from each other with space for your camp.

The cards you've played stay face up in front of you, in the order played. That row is your camp. The card you're playing this round counts as being in your camp; the cards still in your hand don't.

Each round

ChooseRevealApplyCompareScoreCampRepeat

  1. ChooseBoth players pick a card from their hand and place it face down.
  2. RevealFlip together.
  3. Apply card effectsBurn Outs happen first: flip that card upside down, and its ability stops. Then add every bonus from abilities in all camps to each card's printed power.
  4. CompareIf one card beats the other's class, it wins. Otherwise the higher power wins. Equal power is a tie.
  5. ScoreThe winner takes one token from the pile, plus every token in the Temple. On a tie, one token moves from the pile to the Temple and nobody scores.
  6. CampBoth cards stay face up in their owner's camp. Any "If this wins" or "If this loses" abilities happen now.
  7. RepeatPlay the next round, until the last round is done or someone reaches Exodus.

Winning

  • The player with more tokens wins.
  • The moment you hold 3 tokens, it's Exodus: the match ends and you win.
  • After the last round, any tokens left in the Temple are discarded.
  • If the last round ties, the card with the higher printed power wins it and takes the Temple. If printed power is equal too, the round is a draw.
  • If tokens end even, the match is a Whiteout: nobody wins.

Building your hand

  • One copy of any card name.
  • Your hand is fixed: no drawing, no deck.

The class ring

Every card is one of five classes, and every class beats exactly one other, no matter the power.

Because the ring has no top, a power-2 Joker can still knock out a power-9 King. No card is safe, and every reveal is a guess.

The class is printed under the card's name and again on the dark bar under the art. The Beats strip at the top of the rules box tells you which class it beats.

Abilities

No label
Works from the moment the card is played, for as long as it's in your camp. "Your [Build] cards get +2" includes the card itself if it's [Build].
This round:
Only lasts for the round the card is played.
If this wins: / If this loses:
Happens after the round is scored.
"Other"
"Your other [Dance] cards" means the card doesn't count itself.
"Any camp"
Counts cards in every player's camp.
"Your camp gets +1"
Every card in your camp gets it, including the one you just played.
"Your opponent's"
Cards in your opponent's camp, including the card they just played.

Keywords

Burn Out
Flip the card upside down in its camp. Its ability stops working.
Revive
Flip a Burnt Out card face up again. Its ability works again.
Loud
Can't be Burnt Out.
Rogue
A card with no camp (no camp badge). Some abilities count, boost or target Rogues.

Tags

Each card has one or two tags, taken from what the person is known for. Abilities count tags among your allies (your camp), your enemies (your opponent's camp), or both.

Gift Match

Optional stakes, in the spirit of gifting. Agree before the first flip. The loser gifts the winner one card from their camp, loser's choice. A Whiteout means no gift.
